## Deliverable
A requested email is fully prepared, held on final recipients/content/attachments and sent once after exact approval; other communication routes share the gate contract.

## Implementation steps
1. Freeze final-message/action binding and protected adapter semantics, including explicit standing-class exceptions.
2. Use the existing approved Gmail adapter as the first scoped send path; prepare exact sender/recipients/body/attachments without guest secrets.
3. Bind authenticated approval to the actual prepared revision and perform through the protected broker.
4. Specify adapter obligations for other channels and reject unsupported send routes rather than claiming universal channel support.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] An initial request saying send still waits on the final actual message.
- [ ] One exact approval executes once; changed recipients/body/attachments require a new decision.
- [ ] Reports to the authenticated owner are ordinary output; a GitHub exception does not authorize general messaging.
- [ ] Scripts/browser/MCP cannot bypass the held send and no profile bytes/token enter prompts or the VM.
- [ ] Provider unknown outcome is reconciled instead of resending.

## Tests and evidence
1. Prepared-message, stale revision, recipient/attachment substitution and ambiguous approval fixtures -> correct holds.
2. Same action approval replay and crash/provider timeout -> at most one local execution intent plus unknown reconciliation.
3. Alternative tool/session send attempts -> denied by the reviewed protected boundary.
