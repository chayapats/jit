# app.attention-outbox/v1

ROUT14-01 (#255), FR-26/28, NFR-05/10. `S14Attention` in `src/s14/attention.ts` adapts the legacy delivery.outbox/v1 and delivery.attention_levels/v1 algorithms to project-free in-app identities. This phase configures no real channel. `AttentionNotice`, `AttentionPort`, `AttentionCommand`, `AttentionReceipt`, `AttentionSetting` are in routine-types.ts.

`commit(tx,{groupId,class,reason,workId,proposalIds})` commits one durable decision and at most one notice per group; repeats never create another intent. Intermediate sibling completion produces nothing. An owner proposal is visible immediately and shares its occurrence group with the final outcome. Routine reports are informational; failures and decisions are needs_owner; no_update creates no intent. `attention.set` payload `{level:'all'|'needs-me'}` uses setting.revision (initial 0, needs-me). The setting filters future informational intent creation only, not history, existing notices or owner-waiting results. Filtered decisions are retained, preventing later retroactive notices.

`view(actor)` returns `{setting,items:AttentionNotice[]}` (100 newest). This bounded recent-history view is not the open-decision queue. Home/Work use `app.routines.attention(actor)` to select proposed/waiting Work directly over all occurrences and aggregate at most 128 routine groups, regardless of notice reason or age; a later failure escalation cannot hide pending proposals. Delivery failure/unknown aggregates also cover all history. In-app visibility follows the durable intent, separately from optional delivery state. `drain()` marks sent locally with remote:none when no adapter is configured. A trusted injected `AttentionPort.send(notice)` supplies deterministic remote fakes only: sent/unavailable/rejected/unknown. State is queued/claimed/sent/failed/unknown/cancelled; remote is none/accepted/rejected/uncertain. `guarantee:'local_dedupe_only'` never means exactly-once remote delivery. Definitive unavailable allows two safe retries, separated by 1000 ms on the injected clock. Exceptions, unknown results or interrupted claims become unknown and are never retried blindly. A durable independent claim revision fences a stale pre-send image. Raw transport errors are discarded.

`resolve(tx)` observes accepted proposals and cancels queued unsent notices or marks sent/failed/unknown/in-flight notices resolved without claiming withdrawal. Resolved notices lose safe retry. The group retains all proposal IDs and may escalate to failure without duplicating the notice. Queries and GET never dispatch. No real email, Telegram, push or public channel is required or installed.

HTTP and no-script use the shared authenticated foundation command/receipt boundary. See s14-foundation-http.md. UI design/labels remain with Opus, including grouped needs-me and honest unknown delivery. Tests use explicit fakes/barriers and no network.

UX14-02 (#279): `attention.acknowledge` takes exactly `{noticeId}` at notice.revision and returns `{kind,requestId,notice}` through ordinary receipt/retry discipline. It sets resolvedAt, consumes remaining safe retries and cancels only queued delivery. Sent/claimed/failed/unknown states and remote outcomes remain honest; an in-flight send may still settle. Acknowledgement grants nothing, resolves no Work and makes no claim of remote withdrawal. A later new proposal/failure may reopen the same group. Filtered informational groups may escalate to needs_owner; they never suppress a later failure or decision. `attention.set` now affects production routine reports, not only fixture informational notices. It still changes future intent creation only. Default needs-me retains reports in Work/routine history without a new notice.
