Metered routes, effects and reads retain their existing budget gates. No numeric operating allowance, new account or billing permission is invented; unknown measured usage stays unknown.

Durable responsibility belongs to a separate protected journal with an exclusive process lease, original CHAT source/current POLICY authority, deduplicated trigger identities and finite configured stall criteria. Attempts and waits are distinct. Lost attempts reconcile; no native/effect call replays. Transport, execution cessation and historical effect outcome are independent monotonic facts. Scoped stop commits a POLICY/publication fence before cooperative cancellation and retains unsettled capacity. A stall pauses only that responsibility with attempts/evidence/recommendation and reporting headroom; an authenticated same-scope instruction may resume it. The [responsibility contract](../contracts/s15-responsibility-v1.md) and read-only JSON projections implement the F9/F10 data requirements without visual UI. S14 remains default and adoption remains separate.

Remote-verified reconciliation may close a responsibility whose assigned outcome actually completed, even after execution expiry. This factual terminal update is distinct from publishing the old attempt: its publication fence remains latched and no old handle/result is revived. Unknown/uncertain and owner-statement-only reconciliation retains Needs-you/reconcile-only state and cannot certify settlement or grant execution.

## Proposed decisions

1. **Authorize bootstrap before interpretation.** Real owner ingress and current standing bootstrap policy establish purpose, exact account-route/recipient, applicable pins, data scope and finite enforceable reservation before any model, retrieval, image resolution or discovery. Untrusted classification cannot authorize the context needed to perform classification. Original owner bytes and every applicable current owner decision survive authorized context changes; missing material authority holds.
2. **Separate identities and evidence.** Route/provider strings, credential-store addresses, model metadata/catalogs, structural JSON, hashes, memory and source/model output authenticate nobody. Protected registries bind actual owner/workers, request/purpose, Bot/resources, account/billing/lifecycle epoch, recipient and source scope. Model identity is verified against the exact trusted adapter object/mapping, including codex→`codex-subscription` and grok→`xai`. Requested effort, mapped control, provider-reported metadata and inference observation remain separate facts. None proves internal computation.
3. **Select only inside current ceilings.** Ordinary/difficult ordering and evidence rank eligible approved selections. Every explicit route/model/effort/author pin narrows the intersection. A settled known failure may permit a separately admitted approved alternative using the same original work/budget continuity, current context and unchanged account/provider/billing/recipient. An unknown call is reconcile-only; a new key/model cannot evade that claim. Required unavailable Claude authorship holds, without substitution or design work.
4. **Use the public forwarding path and disclose its limits.** Preserve #323's `host.auth.createModel()` → guarded public `Model` → `session.submit()` composition. `submitWithRoute` has no caller-model argument; no before-request hook is invented. The pinned adapter evidence demonstrates that the outer stream guard alone cannot fence every post-credential or internal 401 resend. Proposed MG15-P01 requires a supported normalized credential-free final physical-dispatch boundary owned by Schway; MG15-P02 requires exact route/model effort evidence where a pin needs it. This is a proposal based on inert source/declaration evidence, not a live probe or implemented upstream API. No private import/protocol interceptor or Jit retry loop is introduced.
5. **Charge exact physical children conservatively.** Plans are ceilings; immutable child charges/claims precede I/O. Stream retries/compaction, image/additional reads and delivery cannot consume a paid parent as an uncharged new child. Original registered unconsumed context continues its exact committed charge at zero remaining allowance without double charging. Lost/consumed/recovered contexts do not dispatch. Nonrefundable enforceable finite upper bounds remain separate from measured/partial/unknown usage; subscriptions/null telemetry are never free. MG15-P03 retains the missing monetary-bound requirement, including codex's documented lack of an output-token cap. Cache/reasoning counters are subsets, and repeated cumulative observations are not additive charges.
6. **Keep complete result and cancellation paths.** Native terminal identity and exact retained read lead through sanitized ResultV1, current guarded read and synchronous final publication, then the conversational owner result path. Provisional progress/thinking/raw errors cannot bypass it. Signal and explicit stop use the same memoized original-scope cancel; actual transport/process settlement remains independent. Preserve #321 prepare/accept/signal/readResult/publishResult and #322's action-or-read emitter, exact original child charges, directional complete frames and independent capture/input/network/channel/scope/process facts. Stop/rejected evidence cannot erase prior facts or refund charges.
7. **Retain current knowledge across restart/adoption/rollback.** One current protected continuity authority retains request/receipt/claim/charge identities, usage, withdrawals and policy revocations. Unknown work never blindly replays. Old grants/approvals/routines remain nondispatchable; original S14/legacy bytes/private account/data restrictions and fixed opaque native workspaces remain. No raw native/profile relocation/copy/promotion, inherited numeric budget/readiness, permanent-Bot policy or Jod integration.

## Alternatives and consequences

- Choosing a model first and asking it to authorize its own context spends/discloses before authority exists. Explicit bootstrap and child reservations add bookkeeping and retain an honest hold when scope is missing.
- A provider/id comparison or account label is forgeable and cannot detect a changed billed account. Protected account-route registration and current epoch verification require actual integration evidence.
- Treating every stream as exactly one physical send misses the demonstrated adapter-owned renewal resend and post-credential race. A supported Schway final boundary is required for that stronger guarantee; production governed invocation stays unavailable until proved. A raw fetch override would move credential/protocol concerns into Jit and is excluded.
- Dropping an unsupported effort pin or assuming `off` disables reasoning misreports actual controls. Explicit evidence/refusal can reduce eligible models while preserving owner intent.
- Charging only measured tokens or refunding unknown failures can recreate allowance after a crash. Conservative nonrefundable bounds may exhaust budget sooner; agents cannot increase it or silently switch billing to compensate.
- Returning output from callbacks before current publication checks bypasses stop/source/recipient fences. Retained results and independent facts cost state but preserve truthful partial/unknown outcomes.
