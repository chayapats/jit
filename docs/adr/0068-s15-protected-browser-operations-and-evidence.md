# Protected browser operations, actual dispatch and observed evidence

## Status and inputs

[S15](../specs/S15-conversational-autonomy.md) requires general browser work without bespoke business integrations, owner assistance at login/2FA, mediated team resources and system-enforced human-required gates. A logged-in account is not general send/payment/upload authority. S14 remains the implemented default with Bot-private, network-free computers and scroll-only agent input.

## Proposed decisions

1. **Separate mediated capability from account custody.** Keep profiles/cookies/passwords/2FA/CDP and control authority in a separately protected browser domain, beyond working-guest administration. Resolve endpoint/profile/session/process identity from protected registration and independent verification. Working tools receive only authorized bounded proposals/observations, never a session bearer handle or generic JS/network/control capability. Shared team use preserves explicit restrictions and S14-private scopes.
2. **Version closed browser operations and immutable dispatch.** Specify navigate/read/necessary ordinary interaction/download, exact request/source/recipient/content semantics, finite framing/time/resource limits, current work/attempt/lease/takeover/process/observation generations and request dedupe. Use explicit noncircular operation/request action-payload projections before #320 creates action hashes. Claim/approval/evidence recording does not mutate a prepared action revision/hash.
3. **Make actual effects the support boundary.** A current proven support manifest must cover input/local state and every actual network/credential route. Unknown consequences refuse before the initial input; known supported preparable actions may be held. The protected emitter must own final exact bytes through an externally effective policy/conditional dispatch fence, including redirects, frames, popups, scripts, service workers and alternate guest routes. Generic callbacks, labels, HTTP methods, allowlists or screenshots are insufficient. The actual mechanism and representative usable workflow proof remain unfrozen; a read-only-only final product is not accepted as general browser completion.
4. **Expose response and cancellation paths.** Trusted custom tools use actor-bound prepare/accept, signal-aware dispatch, guarded readResult and synchronous final publishResult. All content comes from the retained correlated response. Cancellation uses memoized original-scope cleanup even after revoke and never reconstructs authority after process loss. Preserve #320's original-context continuation at zero remaining already charged allowance, separate publication guards, nonrefundable upper bounds and null unknown usage.
5. **Keep facts independent and monotonic.** Accepted intent, physical input acknowledgement, actual request/effect dispatch, captured response, cooperative cancel, channel closure, exact operation quiescence, independent process stop and final publication are distinct. A rejected proof cannot erase recorded response/channel/usage facts. Valid stop evidence changes only the exact resource scopes it proves; historical unknown effects stay reconcile-only. Owner statements and copied structural evidence are not management attestation.
6. **Protect login, evidence and downloads.** Login/2FA yields payload-free boundary events and transient protected authenticated owner input. It does not approve agent effects. Observations need an authorized safe surface, actual source lineage and fresh recipient/publication guards; generic masking cannot promise secret removal. Downloads stay bounded/inert/quarantined; no implicit execution, profile export or workspace import. Exact import/release remains a downstream serializer/consent/VM obligation.
7. **Preserve current continuity through recovery and adoption.** Keep request/claim/usage/withdrawal/policy facts in the current protected authority outside guest/browser/native snapshots. Restart reconciles metadata and externally observed scopes without replay. Old grants/approvals/routines remain nondispatchable. No raw native/profile relocation/copy/promotion, inherited allowance/account/model readiness, permanent-Bot policy or Jod integration. Unsupported reverse projection keeps both runtimes fenced.

## Alternatives and consequences

- Reusing S14's offline runtime or legacy label/form classification as general authenticated-browser enforcement would change their meaning and leave alternate effects reachable. Their valid opacity/lease/settlement/observation seams can be reused only within their existing limits.
- Exporting unrestricted CDP, JS, cookies or a logged-in browser to the guest would give it routes around human-required gates. External custody alone is also insufficient without actual effect/disclosure mediation.
- Sending unknown input and waiting to classify its resulting request can already perform local or remote effects. Pre-input support and protected complete interception cost compatibility; unsupported flows must identify the actual missing proof.
- Approving a broad navigation/click cannot authorize arbitrary redirect recipients or script/worker requests. Per-request slots and exact semantics add accounting and partial-progress complexity but preserve already sent parent facts and prevent blind retries.
- Treating abort/socket close/ack as process stop releases capacity too early. Independent scoped proof may be difficult; the truthful result is pending/unknown with held capacity until proof or an authorized exact broader stop, without silently disrupting sibling work.
- The public pinned `schway/host` custom-tool/image/session seams support composition. No verified missing public host contract is demonstrated, so no Schway issue or private import is proposed. Schway retains loop/providers/retries/compaction/native ownership.
